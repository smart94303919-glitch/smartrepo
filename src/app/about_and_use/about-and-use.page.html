About and Use

About SMART

A system that uses smartphone cameras and image-processing technology to automatically scan, check, and record student assessment quickly and accurately.

Use of SMART

It is used to quickly scan, automatically grade and securely store student assessment results, improving efficiency and accuracy in the evaluation process.

Vission

A modern academic environment driven by fast, accurate, and digitally organized assessment tools.

Mission

To provide a fast, accurate, and digitally organized assessment tool for educational institutions.
